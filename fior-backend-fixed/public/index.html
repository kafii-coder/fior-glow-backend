Our Commitment

Refund & Return Policy

Transparent, fair, and customer-first — here's exactly how returns and refunds work at Fior Glow.

1. Refund Eligibility

You may request a return or refund within 7–14 working days of receiving your order. The request must be submitted within this website.

2. Product Condition

Item must be unused/only inspected, in original packaging, should not be damaged, with all accessories, invoice and inserts returned, matching the reported issue.

3. Mandatory Unboxing Video

An unedited, continuous unboxing video is required — recording must start before opening, show the shipping label, and have no cuts. Without it, damage/missing/wrong-item claims may not qualify.

4. Evidence Submission

Please share your unboxing video, product photos, order number, and issue description via a shared Google Drive link or direct upload on our site. Drive links must be accessible.

5. Inspection Process

Our review begins within 1–2 business days of your request. We verify the evidence and may contact you if more info is needed.

6. Return Shipping

If we shipped the wrong, damaged, or defective item — return shipping is on us. If you changed your mind or ordered incorrectly, return shipping is the customer's responsibility.

7. Refund Timeline

Once your return passes inspection, refunds are processed within 7–14 working days, back to your original payment method whenever possible. You'll get email updates throughout.

8. Non-Refundable Items

For hygiene & safety: opened skincare/makeup, used cosmetics, personal care items, gift cards, promotional gifts, and clearance items (unless damaged) cannot be returned once opened.

9. Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled anytime before they are shipped. Once shipped, cancellation is no longer possible.

10. Exchange Policy

Eligible products can be exchanged for the wrong size, wrong variant/shade, wrong item shipped, or a manufacturing defect.

11. Fraud Prevention

Fior Glow reserves the right to reject fraudulent or abusive refund requests. Repeated suspicious behavior may lead to account restrictions.

12. Customer Support

✉️ viexim.bd@gmail.com · 💬 WhatsApp Live Chat · 🕐 Business Hours: 10 AM – 8 PM (Sat–Thu) · Typical response within a few hours.

13. Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a refund take? +

Once your returned item passes inspection, refunds are issued within 7–14 working days to your original payment method.

Can I return opened cosmetics? +

No — for hygiene and safety reasons, opened skincare, makeup, and other used cosmetics cannot be returned or refunded.

Why is an unboxing video required? +

It protects both you and us — it's the clearest proof of the product's real condition and contents at the moment of delivery.

Can I exchange instead of requesting a refund? +

Yes — for a wrong size, wrong shade, wrong item shipped, or a manufacturing defect, we're happy to arrange an exchange.

Who pays the return shipping? +

If the fault was ours (wrong/damaged/defective item), we cover it. If you changed your mind, the shipping cost is yours.

What happens if my Drive link doesn't work? +

Please double-check the sharing permission is set to "Anyone with the link," or upload the evidence directly through our website instead.
